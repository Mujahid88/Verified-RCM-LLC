Pain management: improving quality of life through comprehensive care

Pain is one of the most common reasons people seek medical care. While acute pain often resolves as the body heals, chronic pain can persist for months or years, impacting physical health, emotional well-being and overall quality of life. Pain Management is dedicated to diagnosing, treating and managing pain through comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Conditions commonly treated

  • ✓Chronic back and neck pain, sciatica and nerve pain
  • ✓Arthritis and degenerative disc disease
  • ✓Fibromyalgia and neuropathic pain
  • ✓Migraine and chronic headaches
  • ✓Post-surgical and cancer-related pain

Treatment approaches

Pain Management combines medical, rehabilitative and interventional treatments tailored to each patient:

  • ✓Comprehensive pain assessment and medication management
  • ✓Physical therapy and therapeutic exercise
  • ✓Epidural steroid injections and nerve blocks
  • ✓Radiofrequency ablation and spinal cord stimulation
  • ✓Behavioral health support and lifestyle counseling

Final thoughts

By focusing on both symptom relief and long-term wellness, Pain Management continues to play a vital role in improving health outcomes, restoring independence and enhancing quality of life for patients of all ages.
