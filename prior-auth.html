How our prior authorization workflow actually runs.

Prior authorization delays are one of the top reasons claims stall or get denied. We run a structured, proactive workflow so authorizations are in hand before the appointment, not chased after the fact.

The workflow, step by step

  • ✓1. Eligibility & benefit check: confirm active coverage and authorization requirements before scheduling.
  • ✓2. Requirement lookup: match the CPT/HCPCS code and diagnosis against the specific payer policy.
  • ✓3. Documentation assembly: gather clinical notes, orders and medical necessity justification from the chart.
  • ✓4. Submission: file through the payer portal, fax or phone, whichever that payer requires, with tracking.
  • ✓5. Follow-up: proactive status checks every 48-72 hours instead of waiting on the payer to respond.
  • ✓6. Peer-to-peer coordination: schedule and prep the provider for a physician review if a request is denied.
  • ✓7. Authorization tracking: log the approval number, visit count and expiration date against the schedule.

Why this reduces denials

Most authorization denials come from missing documentation or expired approvals, not medical necessity. Tracking every authorization to its expiration date, and matching visit counts against what was approved, closes that gap before it becomes a denied claim.
