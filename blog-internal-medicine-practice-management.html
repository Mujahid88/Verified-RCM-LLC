Internal medicine practice management: strategies to improve revenue, efficiency and patient care

Internal Medicine physicians manage patients with chronic conditions, preventive care needs and complex medical concerns. While patient care remains the top priority, administrative demands, insurance verification, prior authorizations, coding compliance and billing continue to grow and can significantly impact operational efficiency and financial performance.

Common operational challenges

  • ✓Increasing claim denials and payment delays
  • ✓Complex insurance verification and prior authorization requirements
  • ✓Coding accuracy for Evaluation and Management (E/M) services
  • ✓Managing chronic and preventive care documentation
  • ✓Accounts receivable aging and delayed collections

Building a strong revenue cycle

Every stage, from scheduling and insurance verification to coding, claim submission, payment posting, denial management and A/R follow-up, must work together seamlessly to maintain healthy cash flow and improve clean claim rates.
