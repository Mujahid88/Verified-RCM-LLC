Core service detail

Medical coding: types, modifiers and common CPTs by specialty.

Accurate coding is the single biggest lever on a clean claim rate. Our certified coders translate the chart into the exact codes each payer expects, specialty by specialty, before a claim ever leaves the building.

Types of medical coding we handle

  • ✓CPT (Current Procedural Terminology): the code set for office visits, procedures and services rendered.
  • ✓ICD-10-CM: diagnosis coding that establishes medical necessity for every billed service.
  • ✓HCPCS Level II: supplies, durable medical equipment, drugs and services CPT does not cover.
  • ✓DRG and inpatient coding for facility and hospital-based claims.
  • ✓HCC risk-adjustment coding for Medicare Advantage and value-based contracts.

Modifiers we apply correctly, every time

  • ✓Modifier 25: a significant, separately identifiable E/M service on the day of a procedure.
  • ✓Modifier 59: a distinct procedural service, used to prevent inappropriate bundling denials.
  • ✓Modifier 26 / TC: professional versus technical component splits for imaging and diagnostics.
  • ✓Modifier 50: bilateral procedures billed correctly as a single line with the right multiplier.
  • ✓Modifier 76 / 77: repeat procedures by the same or a different provider on the same day.

Common CPTs by specialty

  • ✓Internal medicine & family medicine: 99213-99215 (established patient E/M), 99395-99396 (preventive visits).
  • ✓Cardiology: 93000 (ECG), 93306 (echocardiogram), 93458 (cardiac catheterization).
  • ✓Physical therapy: 97110 (therapeutic exercise), 97140 (manual therapy), 97530 (therapeutic activities).
  • ✓Mental health: 90837 (60-minute psychotherapy), 90847 (family therapy), 90792 (psychiatric evaluation).
  • ✓Pediatrics: 90460-90461 (immunization administration), 99381-99385 (well-child visits).
