How we contact you, and how to opt out.

We only contact you in the ways below, and you can change your mind at any time.

Replies and service updates

We email you to answer your inquiry and, for clients, to send service updates and reports. We send marketing or promotional email only if you have asked for it, and every such email includes an unsubscribe link.

To opt out: click the unsubscribe link, reply with the word unsubscribe, or email info@verifiedrcm.com. We stop marketing email within 10 business days, usually sooner.

Calls about your request

If you give us a phone number we may call it about your request or your meeting. We do not make automated or prerecorded marketing calls.

To opt out: tell us on the call, or call or email us, and we will add your number to our do-not-call list and stop calling you.

Texts only if you say yes

We text you only if you agree on the form above. We send replies, meeting reminders and follow-ups about your request, never marketing. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out: reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT to any message. Reply HELP for help. See our Messaging Terms and Privacy Policy.
