Legal

Privacy policy.

This policy explains what information Verified RCM collects, how it is used, and the rights you have over it. It applies to visitors of this website and to the practices and patients whose data we process under a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

Information we collect

Contact details submitted through forms on this site (name, practice name, email, phone), and, for client practices, protected health information (PHI) processed solely to perform billing, coding and credentialing services under a signed BAA.

How we use information

Website inquiry data is used only to respond to your request and is never sold. PHI processed on behalf of a client practice is used exclusively to submit claims, verify eligibility, manage denials and report on revenue cycle performance, per HIPAA's minimum-necessary standard.

Data security

All PHI is handled on encrypted, access-controlled systems with audit logging on every touch. Access is limited to staff directly working a given account, each covered by signed confidentiality agreements and annual HIPAA training.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information submitted through this website by contacting us directly. Patients of client practices should direct PHI requests to their provider, who remains the HIPAA-covered entity.
